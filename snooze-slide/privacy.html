Privacy Policy — Snooze Slide

Last updated: 2026-10-27

FirstImmortalStudio ("we") built Snooze Slide (the "App"). This page explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

The App itself does not ask for, collect or store personal information such as your name, email address or location. Game progress and settings are stored on your device, and a copy is kept in your Google Play Games profile and in your phone's Android backup (see below).

Cloud save (Google Play Games)

When you open the App, it signs in to Google Play Games on its own if your phone has a Play Games profile. There is no button and nothing to do. If you have no profile or you are offline, the game simply keeps your progress on the phone.

After sign-in, the App saves a copy of your progress in Google Play Games (Saved Games) and reads it back on another phone or after a reinstall. The copy holds: the level you reached, stars per level, seeds, the items you own and wear, the daily gift date and streak, free hints used today, the language and sound settings, and simple play counters (how many times you opened the game and finished a level). It holds no name, email or contact details.

The copy is stored by Google on its servers, linked to your Play Games profile, and sent over an encrypted connection. Only the App, signed in as you, can read it. We have no server and never receive it. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Android backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android copies the App's small settings files to your Google account: the same game save, your ad consent choice, and any small settings files the ad partners' software keeps in the App. Android does this, not the App. The backup sits in a private area of your Google account that we cannot read, and on Android 9 and newer with a screen lock it is end-to-end encrypted. It is used to restore the game when you set up a new phone or reinstall.

Advertising

The App shows ads. Our advertising partners may collect and process data such as the advertising ID of your device, IP address, device model, operating system version and interactions with ads, to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy options" button in the App's settings.

The data our advertising partners process is: approximate location (derived from the IP address), device identifiers such as the advertising ID, interactions with ads, ad-related purchase signals, and app diagnostics. It is used for advertising, measurement, analytics, app functionality and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Your choices and requests

  • Personalized ads: use the "Privacy options" button in the App's settings at any time.
  • Access or deletion of advertising data: Unity is the controller of the data its ad services process. Use the rights request options in Unity's privacy policy (https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy), or email us and we will help you with the request.
  • Game progress: on your device, in your Play Games profile and in your Android backup. See "How to delete your data" below.

How to delete your data

Snooze Slide has no account of its own. Your progress is on your phone, in your Play Games profile and in your Android backup. Ad data is processed by Unity (LevelPlay and Unity Ads). To delete it:

1. Uninstall Snooze Slide to delete your game progress and settings from the phone. Do this first: while the App is installed, it saves your progress to Play Games again. 2. Delete the cloud save: in the Play Store app tap your profile picture > Settings > General > Play Games Profile > Privacy & Settings > Delete profile & game data, then under "Delete individual game data" find Snooze Slide and tap Delete. On the web: https://play.google.com/games/profile > Your Data. 3. Android backup: turn backup off in your phone settings (Settings > Google > Backup, the path differs a little by phone). Google removes old backups after a while; you can also delete a device backup in the Google One or Google Drive app under Backups. 4. Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads), so earlier ad data is no longer linked to your device. 5. To ask Unity to delete the ad data linked to your device, use the data request options in Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy 6. Or email us at evrdaus@gmail.com with the subject "Snooze Slide data deletion" and we will forward your request to Unity and confirm it within 30 days.

Deleted: game progress (on uninstall), the Play Games cloud save and the Android backup (by you, in your Google settings), and ad data held by Unity (on request, per Unity's policy). We keep nothing ourselves; Unity may keep some data where the law requires it (for example fraud prevention records), as described in its policy.
