Privacy Policy — Basalt Bounce

Last updated: 2026-11-02

FirstImmortalStudio ("we") built Basalt Bounce (the "App"). This page explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

The App itself does not ask for, collect or store personal information such as your name, email address or location. Game progress, the shop, the museum and settings are stored on your device. The App has no account of its own, no login screen and no chat.

Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the App keeps a copy of your game progress (runs played, best depths, shards, upgrades, items and gems found, the daily run) in your Play Games profile, so it comes back after a reinstall or on a new phone. The copy is stored by Google in your Play Games account, not by us; we do not receive it. You can delete it in the Play Games app (Profile > Settings > Delete Play Games account and data) or in your Google Account.

Advertising

The App shows ads. Our advertising partners may collect and process data such as the advertising ID of your device, IP address, device model, operating system version and interactions with ads, to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy options" button in the App's settings.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). To ask the advertising provider to delete data linked to your device, use Unity's data request form: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy (section "Your rights"), or write to the contact below and we will forward the request.

In-app purchases

The App has no in-app purchases.

Third-party services

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource mediation) and Unity Ads — https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google Play Games Services (cloud save) — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Any additional ad network enabled in LevelPlay mediation is listed here before it is enabled.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows when it last changed.
